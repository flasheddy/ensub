Ensub modifies the material by selecting entries with both definitions and
CMUdict pronunciations, retaining up to three ranked definitions, generating
inflected-form mappings, converting pronunciations to IPA, and packaging the
result as a compressed SQLite database. Source and artifact checksums are in
../PROVENANCE.toml.
